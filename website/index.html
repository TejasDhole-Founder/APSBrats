The brat life

Three schools, four cities, one childhood scattered everywhere.

Army kids change postings every few years. By the time you graduate, your closest friends are spread from Patiala to Pune to Delhi Cantt — and the WhatsApp groups always fizzle out. APS Brat is the one place built around how brats actually grew up: by school, section, and batch year.

Fall in, in 3 steps

From stranger to batchmate in a minute.

  1. 01

    Verify your school

    Sign in with your phone — no passwords. Add the APS schools you attended, your class, section and batch years.

  2. 02

    Auto-join your batch

    You're instantly dropped into your exact section and school communities — "12A · APS Patiala · 2022" and more.

  3. 03

    Reconnect & chat

    See who just joined, connect with batchmates, and pick up the conversation right where school left off.
